Harbor View Residences

Multifamily · Seattle, WA · 2024

A coordinated production package developed around the project’s design, schedule, and delivery requirements.

Project Overview

  • Client type: Residential developer
  • Market: Multifamily
  • Location: Seattle, WA
  • Scope: Amenity furniture and wayfinding
  • Quantity: Lobby, clubroom, and outdoor amenity areas
  • Services provided: Package review, sampling, production, delivery
  • Delivery condition: Amenity-area labeled delivery
  • Completion year: 2024
The Requirement

The developer needed coordinated furniture and signage for lobby, leasing, clubroom, and outdoor amenity spaces ahead of a building opening date.

Project Snapshot

  • Furniture and wayfinding package across four amenity areas
  • Key finish samples approved ahead of production
  • Production and delivery coordinated around a fixed building-opening date
  • Amenity-area labeled delivery for lobby, clubroom, leasing, and outdoor spaces
The Carve Approach

Carve Creation reviewed the amenity furniture and signage scope as one package, developed samples for key finishes, and coordinated production and delivery around the opening schedule.

Key Scope Elements

  • Lobby and leasing-office furniture
  • Clubroom and shared workspace furniture
  • Outdoor amenity furniture
  • Directory and wayfinding signage
  • Amenity-area labeled packaging
Outcome

Furniture and signage were delivered and staged by amenity area ahead of the building opening.
