The Carve Flex™ System

Standardized Core. Customized Space.

Carve Flex™ spaces are not designed as one-off structures. They are built on a modular platform that gives you the freedom to create different spaces, functions, and experiences—without starting from scratch every time.

We begin with a carefully engineered foundation of materials, standardized dimensions, and universal connection systems. From there, walls, doors, windows, partitions, finishes, and functional modules can be combined to create a space tailored to its purpose. Instead of redesigning the entire structure for every application, we adapt the space through interchangeable modules and configurations.

Standardized

  • Modular dimensional grid
  • Structural connections
  • Wall-panel system
  • Ceiling and floor approach
  • Lighting, power, data, and selected MEP interfaces
  • Packing and installation logic

Configurable

  • Glass partitions for openness and natural light
  • Sliding walls for flexible spaces
  • Fabric and acoustic partitions for privacy and comfort
  • Solid walls for enclosed environments
  • Standardized door and window systems
  • Integrated ceiling, flooring, and MEP interfaces

  • Technology and equipment
