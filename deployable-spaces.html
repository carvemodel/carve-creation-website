Carve Flex™ Deployable Spaces

Space That Moves With
the Organization.

Traditional interior construction is fixed to one location. When a workplace lease ends or operational needs change, much of that investment may be demolished and discarded.

Carve Flex™ offers a different model: deployable interior environments built from standardized aluminum structural profiles, coordinated component sizes, and reusable connections. The system can be assembled, relocated, reconfigured, and deployed again as organizational needs evolve.

When purchased, Carve Flex™ can remain a reusable corporate asset instead of becoming part of a disposable leasehold improvement. When leased, it can provide access to functional space through a more flexible operating model, reducing the need for a large upfront investment in permanent construction.

From Fixed Construction
to Reusable Space

Carve Flex™ changes how organizations can plan, fund, and manage interior environments.

Reduce Disposable Fit-Out

Conventional meeting rooms, offices, and enclosed spaces are commonly built into a leased property. When the lease ends, they may be demolished even when the organization needs the same functions elsewhere. Carve Flex™ is designed to be disassembled, moved, and used again—reducing material waste and preserving more of the value invested in the space.

Own a Reusable Corporate Asset

When purchased, the system belongs to the organization rather than the building. It can be relocated to another floor or property, adapted to a new use, or reconfigured as workplace requirements change.

Support a More Flexible Operating Model

When leased, Carve Flex™ can reduce the need for a large upfront expenditure on permanent construction and align the commitment more closely with the intended period of use. The exact accounting treatment depends on the agreement and the organization’s accounting policies, but leasing can provide a more flexible alternative to conventional capital-intensive fit-out.

Extend Value Across Multiple Uses

Standardized components can be recovered, maintained, supplemented, and redeployed rather than discarded with each change of location. The same system can support evolving teams, temporary programs, phased developments, and repeated activations over time.

One Platform, Multiple Configurations

Carve Flex™ uses a controlled kit of parts to create different types and sizes of deployable environments without redesigning the underlying system for every project.

What Is Standardized
and What Can Be Configured

Carve Flex™ balances the efficiency of a standardized system with the flexibility needed to support different environments and uses.

Standardized System

The underlying platform is controlled to maintain compatibility, quality, and repeatability. Standardized elements include:

  • Aluminum structural profiles
  • Component dimensions and planning modules
  • Structural connections and assembly details
  • Wall, ceiling, glazing, and door interfaces
  • Integrated channels for lighting, power, data, and technology
  • Base, leveling, and attachment conditions
  • Packaging, handling, and assembly methods

Standardization allows components to be manufactured consistently, installed efficiently, maintained more easily, and reused in future configurations.

Configurable Elements

Projects can be configured from an established range of compatible options, including:

  • Overall layout and number of modules
  • Open, semi-enclosed, or enclosed arrangements
  • Solid, glazed, or partially glazed panels
  • Selected panel materials, colors, and finishes
  • Visual and acoustic privacy levels
  • Doors and access conditions
  • Integrated furniture and storage
  • Lighting, power, data, and technology
  • Branding and environmental graphics

Available options depend on the selected configuration, purchase or lease model, site conditions, and project requirements.

Configuration and Design Support

Adapting the System Without Redesigning It.

Carve Creation helps clients select and configure an appropriate Carve Flex™ solution around the intended use, number of occupants, site conditions, privacy requirements, technology, duration, and deployment model.

Our role is to coordinate project requirements with the established system—not to customize every structural component or connection.

Depending on the project, our support may include:

  • Selecting an appropriate standard size or module arrangement
  • Planning layout, access, and circulation
  • Choosing from available panel, glazing, and finish options
  • Establishing visual and acoustic privacy requirements
  • Coordinating compatible furniture and storage
  • Integrating lighting, power, data, and technology
  • Incorporating branding and environmental graphics
  • Planning transportation, assembly, relocation, and storage
  • Adapting an approved configuration for repeated deployment

Requests outside the standard platform are reviewed individually. They may require additional development, testing, cost, and production time and may not be available under the lease program.

Why Standardization Matters

Carve Flex™ is developed through a DfMA approach in which components are designed for consistent manufacturing, efficient assembly, controlled quality, and future reuse.

Its standardized aluminum-profile platform and repeatable connections help:

  • Reduce project-specific engineering and fabrication
  • Maintain compatibility between components
  • Improve production and installation consistency
  • Limit the amount of work required on-site
  • Simplify replacement and maintenance
  • Support disassembly, relocation, and reconfiguration
  • Improve cost and schedule predictability
  • Enable controlled production across multiple units or locations

The objective is not unlimited customization. It is to provide enough flexibility to respond to different needs while protecting the efficiency and reliability of a standardized system.

How We Work

Carve Flex™ projects follow the same transparent, milestone-based delivery framework used across Carve Creation.

1

Define and Develop

We review the intended use, occupancy, location, duration, site conditions, integrated systems, responsibilities, budget, and schedule. An appropriate standard configuration is then selected and developed into a coordinated basis for pricing, approvals, and manufacturing.

2

Sample and Manufacture

Critical materials, finishes, details, and integrated components are submitted for review at the agreed milestones. Following approval, production proceeds with documented inspections throughout manufacturing and preassembly.

3

Inspect and Deliver

The completed system is inspected against the approved requirements, documented, packaged, and coordinated for delivery and installation according to the agreed deployment plan.

Clients can follow decisions, approvals, milestones, and project records through the Carve Creation project portal.

Information Needed to Begin

Complete drawings are not required for an initial conversation. We can begin by reviewing the intended use and available site information, then identify the most suitable standard configuration.

Helpful starting information includes:

  • Intended use of the space
  • Number of users or expected occupancy
  • Preferred size or available area
  • Number of spaces or units required
  • Desired level of visual and acoustic privacy
  • Furniture and storage requirements
  • Lighting, power, data, and technology needs
  • Heating, cooling, or ventilation expectations
  • Branding and finish preferences
  • Indoor or outdoor placement
  • Site plans, floor plans, photographs, or field dimensions
  • Doorways, elevators, loading areas, and other access conditions
  • Anticipated period of use
  • Purchase or lease preference
  • Potential relocation or repeated-use requirements
  • Project location
  • Target budget
  • Required installation and removal dates

For events or multi-location programs, also provide the deployment schedule, number of locations, frequency of use, storage plan, and expected turnaround between installations.
