Carve Flex™ Deployable Spaces

Space That Moves With
the Organization

Reusable interior environments built from a standardized aluminum-profile system—configured for efficient assembly, relocation, reconfiguration, and purchase or lease.

Discuss a Carve Flex™ Project

From Fixed Construction
to Reusable Space

Traditional interior construction is fixed to one location. When a workplace lease ends or operational requirements change, much of that investment may be demolished and discarded.

Carve Flex™ is designed to be disassembled, relocated, reconfigured, and used again—reducing material waste and preserving more of the value invested in the space.

Own a Reusable Corporate Asset

When purchased, the system belongs to the organization rather than the building. It can be relocated to another floor or property, adapted to a different use, or supplemented with additional components as requirements change.

Support a More Flexible Operating Model

When leased, Carve Flex™ can reduce the need for a large upfront investment in permanent construction and align the commitment more closely with the intended period of use.

The exact accounting treatment depends on the agreement and the organization’s accounting policies. Clients should confirm whether a particular arrangement qualifies as an operating or capital expense with their financial advisers.

A Standardized Platform,
Configured Around the Need

Carve Flex™ is not a completely custom room redesigned for every project. It is a controlled system of compatible components that can be configured around different uses, layouts, locations, and deployment periods.

Standardization protects compatibility, manufacturing consistency, installation efficiency, and future reuse. Configuration provides enough flexibility to respond to the operational and visual requirements of each project.

What Is Standardized

The underlying platform includes:

  • Aluminum structural profiles
  • Component dimensions and planning modules
  • Structural connections and assembly details
  • Wall, ceiling, glazing, and door interfaces
  • Channels for lighting, power, data, and technology
  • Base, leveling, and attachment conditions
  • Packaging, handling, and assembly methods

These elements remain controlled so components can be manufactured consistently, assembled efficiently, maintained more easily, and reused in future configurations.

What Can Be Configured

Projects can be developed from an established range of compatible options, including:

  • Overall layout and number of modules
  • Open, semi-enclosed, or enclosed arrangements
  • Solid, glazed, or partially glazed panels
  • Selected panel materials, colors, and finishes
  • Visual and acoustic privacy levels
  • Door and access conditions
  • Compatible furniture and storage
  • Lighting, power, data, and technology
  • Branding and environmental graphics

Available options depend on the selected configuration, site conditions, intended use, and purchase or lease model.

Requests outside the standard platform are reviewed individually. Nonstandard components may require additional development, engineering, testing, cost, and production time and may not be available under the lease program.

Configuration and Technical Support

Carve Creation helps clients select and coordinate an appropriate Carve Flex™ configuration around the intended use, occupancy, available area, privacy requirements, technology, deployment period, and site conditions.

Our role may include:

  • Selecting an appropriate standard size or module arrangement
  • Planning layout, access, and circulation
  • Coordinating panel, glazing, door, and finish options
  • Establishing visual and acoustic privacy requirements
  • Integrating compatible furniture and storage
  • Coordinating lighting, power, data, and technology
  • Incorporating branding and environmental graphics
  • Planning transportation, assembly, relocation, and storage
  • Adapting an approved configuration for repeated deployment

A DfMA-led approach helps limit project-specific fabrication, reduce on-site work, maintain component compatibility, and improve cost and schedule predictability.

One Platform, Multiple Applications

Carve Flex™ can support different interior environments using the same underlying system.

Enclosed Rooms

Meeting rooms, focus rooms, private offices, consultation rooms, studios, wellness rooms, and other spaces requiring visual or acoustic separation.

Open and Semi-Enclosed Spaces

Team areas, lounges, collaboration spaces, reception points, information centers, and hospitality environments that organize activity without complete enclosure.

Branded and Experiential Environments

Pop-up stores, product showcases, hospitality suites, exhibitions, media rooms, and other environments developed around a particular brand or audience experience.

Multi-Location Deployments

Repeatable configurations for workplace portfolios, phased developments, temporary programs, touring activations, and deployment across multiple properties.

How We Work

Carve Flex™ follows the same transparent, milestone-based delivery framework used across Carve Creation.

1

Define and Approve

We review the intended use, occupancy, location, duration, site conditions, integrated systems, responsibilities, budget, and schedule. The appropriate configuration is then developed and approved as the basis for manufacturing.

2

Manufacture and Verify

Materials, finishes, details, and integrated components are reviewed at the agreed milestones. Following approval, production proceeds with documented inspections and preassembly checks.

3

Deliver and Close Out

The completed system is inspected, documented, packaged, and coordinated for delivery and installation according to the approved deployment plan.

Clients can follow decisions, approvals, production milestones, and logistics through the Carve Creation Project Portal.

Information Needed to Begin

Complete drawings are not required for an initial conversation. Helpful starting information includes:

  • Intended use and expected occupancy
  • Preferred size or available area
  • Number of spaces or units required
  • Desired visual and acoustic privacy
  • Furniture, lighting, power, data, and technology requirements
  • Available floor plans, photographs, or site dimensions
  • Purchase or lease preference
  • Project location, target budget, and required installation date

For repeated or multi-location deployments, also provide the anticipated number of locations, deployment sequence, relocation requirements, and expected period of use.
