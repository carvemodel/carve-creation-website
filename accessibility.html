Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to an accessible website experience.

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Carve Creation Corporation is committed to providing a website experience that is accessible to the broadest possible audience, including people with disabilities.

Our Accessibility Goal

We are working to improve the accessibility and usability of www.carvecreation.com with reference to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA.

Accessibility is an ongoing process. Although we aim to make the Website usable across current browsers, devices, and assistive technologies, we do not claim that every page or feature will be free from accessibility barriers at all times.

What We Are Working On

Our ongoing accessibility work focuses on:

  • Clear page structure and descriptive headings
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and controls
  • Sufficient text and interface contrast
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images
  • Labels and instructions for forms
  • Visible focus indicators
  • Support for zoom and text resizing
  • Reduced-motion preferences where animation is used
  • Descriptive link and button text
  • Accessible error identification and form feedback
  • Periodic review of new and updated content

Assistance and Alternative Access

If you have difficulty accessing any part of the Website or need information in an alternative format, please contact us. We will make reasonable efforts to provide the requested information or assistance through an appropriate alternative method.

When reporting an issue, it is helpful to include:

  • The page address or feature involved
  • A brief description of the difficulty
  • The browser, device, or assistive technology you were using, if known
  • Your preferred method of contact

You are not required to provide disability-related information to request assistance.

Third-Party Content

The Website may contain links to or integrations with third-party websites and services that Carve Creation does not control. We cannot guarantee the accessibility of third-party content, but we welcome feedback when an external service creates a barrier to accessing information or completing an intended task on our Website.

Feedback and Contact

We welcome accessibility feedback and will review reported barriers in good faith.
