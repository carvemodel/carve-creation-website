Carve Project Portal Is Now in Partner Testing

Carve Creation is testing the Carve Creation Project Portal with selected partners ahead of its planned broader launch in January 2027.

By Carve Creation · September 6, 2026

Carve Creation is testing the Carve Creation Project Portal with selected partners ahead of its planned launch in January 2027.

The Portal brings project milestones, files, feedback, approvals, messages, quality-control updates, invoices, and delivery information into one organized workspace.

Testing through active projects

Project-based manufacturing involves many connected decisions—from scope and materials to fabrication, approvals, and delivery. Our current testing program allows selected partners to use the Portal during active projects and provide feedback on how clearly it supports our delivery process.

We are using this period to improve milestone visibility, file exchange, approvals, communication, and the overall client experience before the broader release.

A sample project view, for example, might show a furniture package at 68 percent complete in fabrication, with the next milestone—finishing and quality inspection—due within the week, and the latest quality-control photos already uploaded by the production team. Files, messages, invoices, and approvals for that project sit alongside it, in one place.

Launching January 2027

The Carve Creation Project Portal is planned to launch more broadly in January 2027. Until then, access will remain limited to selected partners participating in the testing program.

Our goal is to make every project easier to follow: what has been decided, what requires attention, what happens next, and where the project stands.
