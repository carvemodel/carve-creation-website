What We Manufacture

From a signature decorative fixture to a coordinated lighting program, we manufacture custom lighting developed around the project’s visual, functional, and technical requirements.

Decorative and Feature Lighting

Pendants, chandeliers, sconces, floor lamps, table lamps, and large-scale statement fixtures that contribute to the identity and atmosphere of a space.

Design Support

From Lighting Concept to Production-Ready Fixture.

Carve Creation can work from an established lighting design or support its further development when construction and production details have not yet been fully resolved.

Our primary role is to translate the intended appearance, scale, light quality, and performance into practical fixture assemblies and production-ready information.

Depending on the project, our support may include:

  • Fixture dimensions and proportions
  • Material and finish development
  • Frame, housing, and enclosure construction
  • Diffusers, lenses, reflectors, and light distribution
  • LED modules, lamps, drivers, and power supplies
  • Color temperature, color rendering, and output coordination
  • Dimming and control-system compatibility
  • Suspension, mounting, and attachment details
  • Access for installation, maintenance, and component replacement
  • Thermal-management and serviceability considerations
  • Sample, mockup, and prototype development
  • Adaptation of a custom fixture for repeatable production

Carve Creation does not replace the project’s lighting designer or electrical engineer. Lighting criteria, circuiting, controls, code compliance, and overall lighting performance should be established or approved by the appropriate project professionals.

Where Our Manufacturing Capability Adds Value

Carve Creation is best suited to lighting projects involving custom forms, specialized materials, integrated illumination, coordinated fixture families, repeated quantities, or multiple manufacturing processes.

Typical fixtures may combine metal, glass, acrylic, stone, wood, fabric, optical components, LED systems, hardware, and specialty finishes within a single assembly.

Our international manufacturing network, DfMA approach, precision equipment, and milestone-based inspections help:

  • Translate lighting concepts into practical fixture assemblies
  • Coordinate structural, decorative, optical, and electrical components
  • Resolve mounting, wiring, ventilation, access, and maintenance requirements
  • Establish approved standards through samples and prototypes
  • Maintain consistent light appearance, materials, and finishes
  • Control repeat production across fixture types and locations
  • Plan packaging and delivery for delicate or oversized components
  • Protect the approved design intent while controlling quality and cost

We create the greatest value when the project requires a custom manufactured lighting solution rather than the selection of a standard catalog fixture.

How We Work

Architectural lighting projects follow the same transparent, milestone-based delivery framework used across Carve Creation.

1

Define and Develop

We review the design intent, fixture types, quantities, lighting criteria, materials, mounting conditions, electrical requirements, responsibilities, budget, and schedule. The available information is then developed into a coordinated basis for pricing, sampling, and manufacturing.

2

Sample and Manufacture

Critical materials, finishes, light sources, optical components, details, and prototypes are submitted for review at the agreed milestones. Following approval, production proceeds with documented inspections throughout manufacturing and assembly.

3

Inspect and Deliver

Completed fixtures are inspected against the approved requirements, documented, identified, protected, packaged, and coordinated for delivery or installation according to the agreed scope.

Clients can follow decisions, approvals, milestones, and project records through the Carve Creation project portal.

Information Needed to Begin

Complete technical documentation is not required for an initial conversation. We can begin by reviewing the available design information and identifying what must be developed next.

Helpful starting information includes:

  • Fixture drawings, sketches, renderings, or reference images
  • Preliminary dimensions
  • Fixture types and estimated quantities
  • Preferred materials, colors, and finishes
  • Intended visual effect and light distribution
  • Required lumen output, if established
  • Color temperature and color-rendering requirements
  • Lamp or LED preferences
  • Dimming protocol and control-system requirements
  • Input voltage and project location
  • Mounting, suspension, and ceiling conditions
  • Indoor, outdoor, wet, or damp-location requirements
  • Certification and testing requirements
  • Architectural and reflected ceiling plans
  • Installation and electrical-connection responsibilities
  • Target budget or pricing objective
  • Required delivery or installation schedule

For repeated or multi-location programs, also provide the anticipated number of locations, fixture schedule, rollout sequence, and expected quantities by phase.
