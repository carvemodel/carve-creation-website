What We Manufacture

From an individual feature fixture to a coordinated lighting family, we manufacture products around the intended appearance, lighting performance, installation conditions, maintenance requirements, and project schedule.

Decorative and Feature Lighting

Chandeliers, pendants, sconces, floor lamps, table lamps, sculptural fixtures, and other decorative elements that contribute to the identity and experience of a space.

Architectural Lighting

Linear fixtures, integrated lighting elements, wall and ceiling features, illuminated architectural components, and other project-specific fixtures coordinated with the surrounding construction.

Large-Scale and Site-Specific Fixtures

Oversized pendants, suspended installations, multi-component fixtures, and custom assemblies requiring coordinated structure, suspension, power, access, transportation, and installation planning.

Coordinated and Repeated Lighting Families

Related fixtures developed with consistent materials, finishes, proportions, light quality, and construction details across different spaces, properties, or locations.

From Lighting Concept
to Production-Ready Fixture

A lighting concept may establish the intended appearance, scale, and light quality without fully resolving how the fixture will be constructed, powered, mounted, serviced, tested, or certified.

Carve Creation coordinates the physical fixture as a complete manufactured assembly, including its structural, decorative, optical, and electrical components. Depending on the project, this may involve light sources, LED modules, drivers, diffusers, lenses, reflectors, controls, mounting systems, internal frames, thermal management, and maintenance access.

Testing and certification can be coordinated when required. Applicable standards, listing methods, responsibilities, costs, and schedules should be established early because they may affect fixture construction and component selection.

Our technical support may include:

  • Scale, proportion, and dimensional development
  • Material and finish coordination
  • Fixture-body and internal-frame development
  • Light-source and LED-module coordination
  • Diffuser, lens, reflector, and optical-component selection
  • Driver, transformer, and power-supply integration
  • Color temperature, color rendering, and output coordination
  • Dimming and control compatibility
  • Canopy, suspension, mounting, and attachment details
  • Heat management and ventilation considerations
  • Access for installation, maintenance, and component replacement
  • Prototype, mockup, and sample development
  • Segmentation for manufacturing and transportation
  • Value engineering without losing the essential design character

When a technical, certification, cost, or schedule condition requires a change, the available options are documented and submitted for review before production proceeds.

Final dimensions, materials, finishes, lighting components, construction details, and prototypes are approved at the agreed project milestones.

Where Our Manufacturing Capability Adds Value

Carve Creation is best suited to lighting projects involving custom geometry, specialized materials, integrated architectural conditions, coordinated fixture families, repeated quantities, or multiple fabrication processes.

A single fixture may combine fabricated metal, glass, acrylic, stone, wood, textiles, diffusers, LED components, drivers, controls, suspension systems, and specialty finishes. These elements must function as one coordinated product while maintaining the intended appearance.

Our DfMA approach, specialized manufacturing network, precision equipment, and milestone-based inspections help:

  • Translate complex designs into practical fixture assemblies
  • Coordinate decorative materials with electrical components
  • Resolve internal structures, connections, and mounting conditions
  • Establish finish and lighting standards through samples and prototypes
  • Identify assembly or performance issues before full production
  • Maintain consistency across repeated fixtures
  • Coordinate testing and certification requirements
  • Plan packaging, transportation, lifting, and installation
  • Support maintenance and future component replacement

We create the greatest value when the lighting requires more than the selection and purchase of a standard catalog fixture.

How We Work

Lighting projects follow the same transparent, milestone-based delivery framework used across Carve Creation.

1

Define and Approve

We review the design intent, fixture schedule, quantities, materials, lighting requirements, mounting conditions, certification criteria, responsibilities, budget, and schedule. The available information is developed into an approved basis for pricing, sampling, and manufacturing.

2

Manufacture and Verify

Critical materials, finishes, lighting components, details, mockups, and prototypes are reviewed at the agreed milestones. Following approval, production proceeds with documented inspections, assembly checks, and required testing.

3

Deliver and Close Out

Completed fixtures are inspected against the approved requirements, documented, identified, protected, packaged, and coordinated for delivery or installation according to the agreed scope.

Clients can follow drawings, samples, approvals, production milestones, testing records, and logistics through the Carve Creation Project Portal.

Information Needed to Begin

Complete construction documents are not required for an initial review. Helpful starting information includes:

  • Lighting drawings, sketches, renderings, or reference images
  • Preliminary fixture dimensions and quantities
  • Preferred materials, finishes, and colors
  • Light-source, color-temperature, and dimming requirements
  • Mounting, suspension, and power conditions
  • Testing or certification requirements
  • Project location and delivery conditions
  • Target budget and required delivery or installation date

For repeated or multi-location programs, also provide the anticipated locations, rollout sequence, quantities by phase, and permitted site-specific variations.
