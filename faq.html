Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about scope, production, samples, delivery, and how project reviews work.

Carve Creation manages project-based manufacturing for contract furniture, branding and wayfinding systems, Carve Flex™ adaptable interior systems, and selected custom products for the built environment.

Carve Creation is primarily project-based. We generally manufacture against an approved scope, quantity, schedule, and delivery requirement rather than maintaining broad U.S. inventory.

Minimums vary by product, material, production method, and project location. We evaluate overall package value and production fit rather than applying one minimum to every scope. Small replacement orders and very small material quantities are generally not a fit.

We can review early information, but a concept is not automatically production-ready. The project may require a paid development, technical coordination, sampling, or prototyping phase before manufacturing can be priced or approved.

We can provide product development and production coordination when included in the scope. Architectural, interior design, engineering, code consulting, and permitting services are not included unless expressly stated and may require appropriately licensed third parties.

Production drawings or submittal information may be included depending on the project. The proposal will define the drawing level, review purpose, approval responsibility, and whether the information is suitable for fabrication, coordination, or reference only.

Yes, subject to material and process limitations. Final standards should be established through a recognized color reference, manufacturer code, physical control sample, or approved finish sample. Screen images and verbal descriptions are not reliable final production standards.

Yes, when appropriate and included in the project scope. Options may include material samples, finish samples, component mockups, first articles, or full prototypes.

Production begins after the applicable scope, drawings, samples, finishes, quantities, commercial terms, and required approvals are complete. The exact production trigger is defined in the proposal.

Change requests can be reviewed, but they may affect price, schedule, materials, tooling, completed work, or feasibility. Some changes become irreversible after a particular production stage.

The quality-control plan depends on the project and may include sample approval, first-article review, in-process inspection, dimension and finish checks, assembly testing, quantity verification, photo documentation, or third-party inspection.

Installation is included only when expressly stated. For signage, Carve Creation does not perform field verification or installation unless a project-specific proposal explicitly says otherwise. The responsible local team must confirm site dimensions, mounting conditions, code requirements, and final locations.

International delivery can be evaluated based on product type, package size, destination, schedule, import requirements, and agreed delivery terms. Responsibilities for freight, customs, taxes, unloading, storage, and last-mile delivery are defined for each project.

Submit the drawings, specifications, quantities, project location, current stage, and required delivery date through the project review form. The more complete the information, the more meaningful our review can be.

We review the scope for fit and completeness. We may request clarification, propose a paid development or prototype stage, prepare a project-specific proposal, redirect the request to another appropriate Carve capability, or decline if the opportunity is not aligned.
