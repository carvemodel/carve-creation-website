Branding & Wayfinding

Design Intent,
Translated Into Physical Systems.

Carve Creation manufactures coordinated branding and wayfinding systems for commercial, hospitality, residential, institutional, and public environments.

We work from established designs or help develop early concepts into production-ready solutions—coordinating materials, construction, illumination, prototypes, repeated production, quality control, packaging, and delivery through one managed process.

What We Manufacture

From a signature identity feature to a complete sign family, we manufacture interior and exterior elements that express the approved design consistently throughout a project.

Branding Systems

Dimensional letters and logos, illuminated identity features, feature walls, architectural brand elements, entrance features, tenant identification, and other custom components that translate a visual identity into the built environment.

Wayfinding Systems

Directional signs, identification signs, directories, maps, room and amenity signs, regulatory signs, parking guidance, and coordinated sign families that help people understand and navigate a place.

Illuminated Signage

Internally illuminated and halo-illuminated letters, logos, cabinets, lightboxes, directories, and other features with coordinated LED components, power-supply access, mounting, and service requirements.

Multi-Location Programs

Prototype-led production for hospitality groups, workplace portfolios, residential developments, retailers, and other organizations requiring consistent signage across multiple buildings or locations.

Design Support

From Approved Concepts to Production-Ready Information.

Carve Creation can work from an established branding or wayfinding design or support its further development when fabrication details have not yet been resolved.

Our primary role is to translate the intended visual language, information hierarchy, and user experience into manufacturable sign types, materials, assemblies, and production documents.

Depending on the project, our support may include:

  • Sign-family and construction development
  • Material and finish coordination
  • Dimensional and proportion review
  • Mounting and attachment details
  • Illumination and electrical integration
  • Message-schedule coordination
  • Accessibility and tactile-signage detailing
  • Prototype and sample development
  • Value engineering without losing the essential design character
  • Adaptation of an approved design for repeated or multi-location production

Final messages, artwork, materials, finishes, construction details, and prototypes are submitted for approval at the agreed project milestones.

Where Our Manufacturing Capability Adds Value

Carve Creation is best suited to branding and wayfinding programs involving dimensional fabrication, custom materials, illumination, integrated hardware, repeated sign families, or coordinated production across multiple locations.

Typical projects may combine fabricated metal, acrylic, wood, glass, stone, composites, graphics, lighting, and custom finishes within one coordinated system.

Our international manufacturing network, DfMA approach, precision equipment, and milestone-based inspections help:

  • Translate design concepts into practical sign assemblies
  • Coordinate multiple materials and fabrication processes
  • Resolve mounting, illumination, access, and service requirements
  • Establish approved standards through samples and prototypes
  • Maintain consistent construction, colors, and finishes
  • Control repeat production across sign types and locations
  • Organize products for efficient distribution and installation

Wall graphics, films, murals, maps, and other surface-applied elements can be coordinated when they support the broader branding or wayfinding system. They are not offered independently as graphic-design, print-only, vinyl-installation, or promotional-signage services.

We create the greatest value when the project requires a coordinated manufactured system rather than an individual commodity sign.

How We Work

Branding and wayfinding projects follow the same transparent, milestone-based delivery framework used across Carve Creation.

1

Define and Develop

We review the design intent, sign types, quantities, messages, site conditions, performance requirements, responsibilities, budget, and schedule. The available information is then developed into a coordinated basis for pricing, sampling, and manufacturing.

2

Sample and Manufacture

Critical materials, colors, finishes, illumination details, and prototypes are submitted for review at the agreed milestones. Following approval, production proceeds with documented inspections throughout manufacturing.

3

Inspect and Deliver

Completed products are inspected against the approved requirements, documented, identified by sign type and location, packaged, and coordinated for delivery or installation according to the agreed scope.

Clients can follow decisions, approvals, milestones, and project records through the Carve Creation project portal.

Information Needed to Begin

Complete construction documents are not required for an initial conversation. We can begin by reviewing the available design information and identifying what must be developed next.

Helpful starting information includes:

  • Branding or wayfinding design package
  • Sign-type drawings, sketches, or renderings
  • Sign schedule and preliminary quantities
  • Message schedule or content requirements
  • Location plans and architectural drawings
  • Preferred dimensions, materials, colors, and finishes
  • Illumination and electrical requirements
  • Accessibility or other applicable performance criteria
  • Interior and exterior exposure conditions
  • Available site dimensions or field surveys
  • Installation responsibilities and site-access conditions
  • Project location and delivery address
  • Target budget or pricing objective
  • Required delivery or installation schedule

For multi-location programs, also provide the number of locations, site-specific variations, rollout sequence, and estimated quantities by phase.
