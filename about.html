Standardized Delivery

Customized in Design. Structured in Delivery.

Every project follows a defined framework—from preliminary pricing and samples through approvals, production, quality verification, and delivery.

Clients receive documented decisions, clear deliverables, and recorded progress at every stage.

1

Preliminary Price Aligned

We review the available scope, quantities, specifications, delivery requirements, and known compliance needs to prepare preliminary pricing.
2

Drawings and Samples Approved

We coordinate product drawings, physical samples, finishes, technical requirements, testing criteria, and necessary revisions.
3

Agreement Confirmed

We consolidate the approved quantities, drawings, samples, commercial terms, responsibilities, testing requirements, schedule, and delivery conditions.
4

Production Released

We verify that all approved information is complete and issue one controlled manufacturing package to the production team.
5

Production Managed

We coordinate procurement and manufacturing while recording and communicating progress at agreed milestones.
6

Quality and Compliance Verified

We inspect and test completed products against the approved drawings, physical samples, specifications, and acceptance criteria.
7

Delivery Coordinated

We coordinate packaging, freight, tracking, receiving conditions, site access, and agreed installation responsibilities.
8

Closeout Completed

We document delivery, resolve agreed punch items, and provide warranty, maintenance, and final project records.
