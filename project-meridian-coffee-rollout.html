Meridian Coffee Rollout

Retail, Food & Beverage · Denver, CO · 2025

A coordinated production package developed around the project’s design, schedule, and delivery requirements.

Project Overview
  • Client type: Multi-location café brand
  • Market: Retail, Food & Beverage
  • Location: Denver, CO
  • Scope: Branded interior signage and wayfinding package
  • Quantity: Multi-location rollout program
  • Services provided: Standards development, sampling, production, quality control, delivery
  • Delivery condition: Location-labeled, ready-to-install delivery
  • Completion year: 2025
The Requirement

The brand required a controlled interior signage and wayfinding standard that could be reproduced consistently across new locations with different footprints, while preserving the approved design intent.

Project Snapshot
  • Interior identification and menu-board signage family
  • Wayfinding and directional elements for a repeatable floor plan
  • Control samples required before rollout production
  • Location-based packaging and labeling for multi-site delivery
  • Phased production aligned to store opening schedules
The Carve Approach

Carve Creation reviewed the brand standards and site conditions, developed control samples to confirm materials and finishes, and managed production and delivery against each location’s opening date.

Key Scope Elements
  • Dimensional and illuminated interior signage
  • Directional and menu wayfinding elements
  • Finish and material control samples
  • First-article inspection for each new location
  • Location-labeled packaging and delivery coordination
Outcome

The signage and wayfinding standard was reproduced across multiple new locations while maintaining a consistent brand identity and finish quality.
