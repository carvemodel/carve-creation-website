Artist Identification and Design Support

From Project Ambition to Fabricated Artwork.

Carve Creation can support a project at different stages. We can collaborate with an already-appointed artist, help identify potential artists, or develop an approved concept into fabrication-ready information.

Artist Identification

When a project has a defined site, objective, or creative direction but no artist has been selected, Carve Creation can identify suitable artists for the client’s consideration.

Potential collaborators may be evaluated according to:

  • Artistic style and creative approach
  • Relationship to the site, community, or project narrative
  • Experience with the proposed scale and materials
  • Suitability for interior, exterior, or public-facing environments
  • Budget and schedule expectations
  • Ability to collaborate through technical development and fabrication

The client retains final responsibility for artist selection and appointment. The artist’s creative scope, compensation, intellectual-property rights, and approval authority are established separately for each project.

Design Development and Fabrication Coordination

Once an artist or artistic direction has been selected, our primary role is to protect the creative intent while resolving the technical information needed to manufacture, transport, assemble, and install the work.

Depending on the project, our support may include:

  • Coordination among the artist, client, design team, engineers, and fabricators
  • Scale, proportion, and geometry development
  • Digital modeling and fabrication documentation
  • Material and finish studies
  • Structural and connection coordination
  • Internal framework and support development
  • Segmentation and assembly planning
  • Anchoring and foundation interfaces
  • Lighting, power, and movement integration
  • Exterior durability and weathering considerations
  • Access for maintenance and component replacement
  • Mockups, samples, and prototype development
  • Transportation, lifting, and installation planning
  • Value engineering without losing the essential artistic character

Carve Creation does not assume authorship of an artist’s work. Artistic control, engineering responsibility, site approvals, and intellectual-property rights are defined with the project team before fabrication begins.

Where Our Manufacturing Capability Adds Value

Carve Creation is best suited to artworks requiring custom geometry, specialized materials, complex assemblies, large dimensions, or coordination across multiple fabrication processes.

Projects may combine fabricated metal, cast components, composites, acrylic, wood, glass, stone, lighting, mechanical systems, and specialty finishes within a single work.

Our international manufacturing network, DfMA approach, precision equipment, and milestone-based inspections help:

  • Translate complex forms into practical fabrication methods
  • Coordinate multiple materials and specialist processes
  • Resolve internal structures, connections, and assembly interfaces
  • Divide large works for manufacturing and transportation
  • Establish appearance standards through samples and mockups
  • Maintain dimensional and finish consistency across components
  • Plan lifting, packaging, delivery, and on-site assembly
  • Protect the approved artistic intent while controlling quality and cost

We create the greatest value when the work requires technical development and specialized manufacturing beyond conventional decorative fabrication.

How We Work

Public art and sculpture projects follow the same transparent, milestone-based delivery framework used across Carve Creation.

1

Define and Develop

We review the project ambition, artistic intent, site conditions, dimensions, materials, structural requirements, responsibilities, budget, and schedule. If an artist has not been appointed, suitable collaborators may be identified for the client’s consideration. The selected concept is then developed into a coordinated basis for pricing, approvals, engineering, and fabrication.

2

Sample and Manufacture

Critical materials, colors, finishes, details, mockups, and prototypes are submitted for review at the agreed milestones. Following approval, fabrication proceeds with documented inspections throughout production and assembly.

3

Inspect and Deliver

The completed work is inspected against the approved requirements, documented, protected, packaged, and coordinated for delivery and installation according to the agreed plan.

Clients can follow decisions, approvals, milestones, and project records through the Carve Creation project portal.

Information Needed to Begin

A selected artist or completed design is not required for an initial conversation. We can begin by reviewing the project ambition, site, available budget, and desired experience.

Helpful starting information includes:

  • Project objective or commissioning brief
  • Whether an artist has already been appointed
  • Preferred artistic direction or reference images
  • Information about the site, community, or project narrative
  • Concept drawings, sketches, renderings, or digital models, if available
  • Preferred dimensions and overall scale
  • Proposed materials, colors, or finishes
  • Interior or exterior placement
  • Intended viewing distance and level of public interaction
  • Site plans, architectural drawings, photographs, or surveys
  • Structural support, anchoring, or foundation information
  • Lighting, power, movement, or technology requirements
  • Environmental exposure and expected service life
  • Maintenance expectations
  • Engineering and permitting responsibilities
  • Access, lifting, and installation conditions
  • Project location
  • Target budget or funding allowance
  • Required completion or installation date
  • Artist, designer, architect, client, and contractor responsibilities

For repeated editions or multi-location installations, also provide the anticipated quantity, acceptable variations, rollout sequence, and approval process.
