Overview

From Concept to Fabricated Artwork.

We collaborate with artists, designers, architects, developers, and public agencies to coordinate design development, engineering, prototyping, fabrication, logistics, and installation planning.

Our Capabilities

  • Site-specific public sculpture
  • Artist-designed fabrication
  • Architectural ornament
  • Integrated art and placemaking elements
  • Commemorative and interpretive installations
  • Custom metal, composite, wood, and mixed-media fabrication
  • Full-scale prototypes and mockups
  • Structural engineering and installation coordination
Design-to-Fabrication Support

We help translate creative concepts into fabrication-ready solutions while preserving the original design intent. Our DfMA approach considers material selection, structural requirements, production methods, transportation, assembly, maintenance, and long-term durability from the beginning.

Prototype Before Production

Complex forms, finishes, connections, and installation details can be evaluated through physical models, material samples, mockups, and full-scale prototypes before final fabrication.

Coordinated Delivery

Every project is managed as a complete manufacturing process—from design development and shop drawings through quality control, packaging, transportation, and site coordination.
