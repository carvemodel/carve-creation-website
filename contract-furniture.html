Design + Manufacturing

Design Intent Meets Manufacturing Discipline.

We understand how furniture should belong to an environment—and how materials, details, construction, packaging, and logistics affect the finished result.

Coherent Design

Proportion, material, color, finish, and detail are coordinated across the furniture family.

Production-Ready Detail

Design information is translated into manufacturable components, assemblies, and specifications.

Controlled Delivery

Samples, approvals, production, quality checks, packaging, and delivery follow one approved design basis.

4

Manufacture

Coordinated factory production against approved information.

5

Inspect & Deliver
