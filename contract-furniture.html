Design Support

From Design Intent
to Production-Ready Information.

Carve Creation can work from an established furniture design or support its further development when fabrication details have not yet been fully resolved.

Our primary role is to translate the intended appearance, experience, and performance into practical materials, assemblies, and production-ready information.

Depending on the project, our support may include:

  • Dimensional and ergonomic review
  • Material and finish development
  • Joinery and construction detailing
  • Hardware and component coordination
  • Upholstery and soft-goods development
  • Engineering and load-related coordination
  • Prototype and sample development
  • Value engineering without losing the essential design character
  • Adaptation of a custom design for repeatable production

Final designs, materials, finishes, and prototypes are submitted for approval at the agreed project milestones.

Where Our Manufacturing Capability Adds Value

Carve Creation is best suited to furniture projects involving custom construction, specialized materials, coordinated product families, repeated quantities, or multiple manufacturing processes.

Typical projects may combine wood, metal, stone, glass, upholstery, lighting, hardware, and specialty finishes within a single collection or environment.

Our international manufacturing network, DfMA approach, precision equipment, and milestone-based inspections help:

  • Translate complex designs into practical assemblies
  • Coordinate multiple materials and production disciplines
  • Establish approved standards through samples and prototypes
  • Maintain consistency across repeated products
  • Identify production issues before full fabrication
  • Control quality, schedule, and cost across the complete program
  • Prepare products for efficient delivery and installation

We are most valuable when the furniture requires more than the selection and purchase of a standard catalog product.

How We Work

Furniture projects follow the same transparent, milestone-based delivery framework used across Carve Creation.

1

Define and Develop

We review the design intent, quantities, performance requirements, materials, site conditions, responsibilities, budget, and schedule. The available information is then developed into a coordinated basis for pricing, sampling, and manufacturing.

2

Sample and Manufacture

Critical materials, finishes, details, and prototypes are submitted for review at the agreed milestones. Following approval, production proceeds with documented inspections throughout manufacturing.

3

Inspect and Deliver

Completed products are inspected against the approved requirements, documented, identified, packaged, and coordinated for delivery or installation according to the agreed scope.

Clients can follow decisions, approvals, milestones, and project records through the Carve Creation project portal.

Information Needed to Begin

Complete construction documents are not required for an initial conversation. We can begin by reviewing the available design information and identifying what must be developed next.

Helpful starting information includes:

  • Furniture drawings, sketches, renderings, or reference images
  • Preliminary dimensions
  • Product types and estimated quantities
  • Preferred materials, finishes, and colors
  • Upholstery or COM/COL requirements
  • Functional and performance expectations
  • Prototype or sample requirements
  • Project location and delivery address
  • Target budget or pricing objective
  • Required delivery or installation schedule
  • Available architectural drawings and relevant site conditions

For repeated or multi-location programs, also provide the anticipated number of locations, rollout sequence, and expected quantities by phase.
