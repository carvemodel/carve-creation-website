Brand Green is sampled from the hero photo's plant foliage and used only for the market-card :target highlight ring. Success is used only for form-confirmation states. Neither appears anywhere else on the site — do not use them as a general accent.

Typography

One typeface across the whole site — Inter, used for both headings and body. Headings are 700 weight with tight tracking; body copy runs in Muted gray at 1.6 line-height.
